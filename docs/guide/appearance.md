# Appearance

Choose how Melodex looks under **Settings → Appearance**. It's kept with your account, so it follows you to every
device you sign in on, and everyone chooses their own.

- **Colour**: pink (Melodex's own), green, blue, grey, purple or amber.
- **Light or dark**: *Auto* follows your device, *Light* and *Dark* stay put, and *Black* is dark with pure black
  behind, for OLED screens.
- **High contrast**: stronger text and borders, and the owned, partial, covered and missing colours in a palette that
  people with the common kinds of colour blindness can tell apart.
- **Long lists**: how many of the Library's artists and albums are shown at first, and then more each time you scroll
  near the end. 60 to start with; fewer opens long lists faster. Lists of songs (the Library's tracks, a playlist, the
  queue) don't need it: however long they are, only the songs near the screen are drawn, and the rest fill in as you
  scroll to them.

The sign-in page uses the last theme chosen in that browser.
